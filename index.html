Research

Design, Modeling, and Experimental Assessment of Heat Exchanger-Based Reflux Batch Distillation System for Ethanol–Water Separation

Under review (third round), Journal of Thermal Engineering | June 2024 – August 2026

Designed and fabricated a three-tray bubble-cap batch distillation system with a dual-condenser reflux arrangement. Built a Python dynamic model using NRTL thermodynamics, equilibrium-stage analysis and energy balances to estimate heating power and reflux ratio without direct flow measurements.

In the primary run, the model predicted 83.70% v/v distillate at 1,094.49 mL, against 79% v/v and 1,049 mL measured. Across three test runs, NRMSE was 6.66% for concentration and 7.83% for volume.

A degenerated-continuum Ritz formulation for the free vibration of rotating pre-twisted, tapered and cambered NACA compressor blades

Manuscript in preparation | June 2025 – ongoing

Developed a semi-analytical five-field First-Order Shear Deformation Theory (FSDT) shell model using the Rayleigh–Ritz method for a rotating NACA 6412 compressor blade. Jacobi-weighted span functions and Legendre chord functions enforce the clamped-root boundary condition. The formulation incorporates pre-twist, stagger, camber, chord and thickness taper, hub radius, and rotational effects, including centrifugal, Coriolis and prestress stiffness.

Implemented in Python, the model is undergoing comparison with an ANSYS finite element model, with preliminary natural frequency results agreeing to within 1.5% for the modes compared.
