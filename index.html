Research

Design, Modeling, and Experimental Assessment of Heat Exchanger-Based Reflux Batch Distillation System for Ethanol–Water Separation

Manuscript under review Journal of Thermal Engineering · Pulchowk Campus · June 2024 – April 2025

  • Designed the complete batch distillation apparatus in SolidWorks, including the boiler, bubble-cap trays, reflux system and condenser.
  • Fabricated and assembled the system using copper brazing, coordinating the manufacturing and integration of components.
  • Conducted performance tests and analysed separation efficiency under varying operating conditions.
  • Developed a Python model to evaluate system performance, validated against experimental data and Aspen Plus simulations.
